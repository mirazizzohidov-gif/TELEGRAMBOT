Mixpodarok Poster — Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Mixpodarok Poster ("the App") is an internal publishing tool operated by Mixpodarok, a gift shop in Tashkent, Uzbekistan (mixpodarok.uz). The App lets the shop's authorised staff publish short product videos of the shop's own catalogue to the shop's own social media accounts, including TikTok.

1. Who may use the App

The App is used only by Mixpodarok and its authorised staff to manage Mixpodarok's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. TikTok integration

When you connect a TikTok account, you authorise the App, through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, to read basic profile information and to upload and publish videos to that account. Before a video is published, the App shows the caption, privacy level and interaction settings, and the account owner chooses them. You can disconnect the TikTok account at any time from the App's dashboard or from TikTok's settings.

3. Content

All published content consists of Mixpodarok's own product photos, videos and descriptions. You are responsible for ensuring that published content complies with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Availability

The App is provided "as is", without warranties. We may change or discontinue the App at any time.
