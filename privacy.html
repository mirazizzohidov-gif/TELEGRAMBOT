Mixpodarok Poster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Mixpodarok Poster ("the App"), operated by Mixpodarok (mixpodarok.uz), handles data when a TikTok account is connected.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) — to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens — to upload and publish videos on the connected account's behalf.
  • Publish status of videos the App uploaded — to show whether a post succeeded.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to publish Mixpodarok's own product videos to the connected TikTok account and to show the status in the App's dashboard. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in transit and at rest in our private database and are accessible only to the App. They are deleted when the account is disconnected.

4. Your choices

You can disconnect the TikTok account at any time from the App's dashboard ("Disconnect") or by revoking access in TikTok's settings; all stored TikTok data is then deleted.
