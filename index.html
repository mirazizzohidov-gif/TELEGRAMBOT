🎁 Mixpodarok Shop Bot

Internal back-office tool of MIXPODAROK LLC (MIXPODAROK MASULIYATI CHEKLANGAN JAMIYAT), a gift shop in Tashkent, Uzbekistan — mixpodarok.uz.

The tool is used only by our owner and staff to manage our own channels:

  • publish our products to our own Telegram channel, Instagram, Facebook Page, YouTube, TikTok, Threads and Pinterest accounts;
  • answer our customers in Instagram Direct and comments with the price and availability of a product;
  • show our staff statistics of our own accounts.

There is no public sign-up. Staff use a private dashboard with a personal access link.
